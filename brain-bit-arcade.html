This Privacy Policy explains how Brain Bit Arcade — Offline Mini Puzzle Arcade ("Brain Bit Arcade," "the App," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Android application.

Brain Bit Arcade is an offline, single-player puzzle game designed for a general audience aged 13 and older. The App includes mini-games such as Mental Math, Number Sequence, Memory Grid, Odd One Out, Word Scramble, Quick Reaction, Schulte Table, Logic Match, and a Daily Challenge.

Brain Bit Arcade does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other directly identifying information.

Although Brain Bit Arcade's gameplay data is stored locally on your device, the App uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to manage advertising consent and privacy choices. These Google services may process certain device and advertising information as described below.

By using Brain Bit Arcade, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information Collection

1.1 Information We Do Not Directly Collect

Brain Bit Arcade does not require or directly collect:

  • Your name; Email address; Phone number; Mailing address;
  • Username or password; User account information;
  • Precise GPS location; Contacts; Camera data; Microphone recordings;
  • Photos, videos, or media files; Payment information; or Biometric information.

There is no registration, login, or user-account system in Brain Bit Arcade.

1.2 Local Gameplay Data

Brain Bit Arcade stores gameplay and preference information locally on your Android device using Android DataStore Preferences.

This information may include:

  • Puzzle scores; High scores; Level and experience points (XP); Achievements;
  • Daily Challenge progress; Gameplay streaks; Game statistics;
  • Sound and haptic settings; Notification preferences;
  • Advertising/privacy preference indicators where applicable; and other game settings and preferences.

This gameplay information is used only to provide and maintain the features of the App. Brain Bit Arcade does not intentionally transmit your locally stored gameplay progress, scores, achievements, XP, streaks, or game settings to our own external server, cloud database, or developer-controlled analytics service.

1.3 Information Processed by Advertising Services

Brain Bit Arcade uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK / Google AdMob to provide banner advertisements, interstitial advertisements between appropriate game sessions, and optional rewarded advertisements that may provide in-game benefits such as hints.

Depending on your device, privacy choices, region, Google configuration, and applicable law, Google and participating advertising providers may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • Android Advertising ID (GAID/AAID), when available and permitted;
  • App Set ID or other device/app identifiers; IP address;
  • Approximate or coarse location inferred from an IP address;
  • Device and operating-system information; Network and connection information;
  • Ad impressions and advertisements shown; Ad interactions, such as taps or video views;
  • App-launch or product-interaction information; Crash information; Performance information;
  • Diagnostic information; and Information used for advertising, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

We do not use this advertising information to create our own user profiles or to associate it with your locally stored Brain Bit Arcade scores or achievements.

1.4 Consent and Privacy Preference Information

Brain Bit Arcade uses the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to display privacy and consent messages where required.

The UMP SDK may process or locally store information relating to:

  • Whether a consent message is required; Your advertising consent choices; Your privacy selections;
  • Applicable regulatory-region information; Consent status; and Signals communicated to Google and participating advertising providers.

These choices may affect whether personalized, non-personalized, or otherwise restricted advertising is eligible to be displayed.

2. How We Use Information

Local Gameplay Information is used to:

  • Save your progress; Maintain scores and high scores; Track XP and levels; Record achievements;
  • Maintain Daily Challenge progress and streaks; Save game preferences; Enable optional haptic feedback;
  • Manage local Daily Challenge reminder preferences; and Restore the expected game state when you reopen the App.

Advertising and Related Information may be used to:

  • Deliver advertisements; Determine whether ads can be personalized; Provide non-personalized or limited advertisements;
  • Measure ad performance; Detect invalid traffic, abuse, and fraud; Diagnose crashes and technical problems;
  • Improve advertising-service reliability; and Comply with applicable privacy and advertising regulations.

We do not use advertising identifiers to identify you personally by name.
